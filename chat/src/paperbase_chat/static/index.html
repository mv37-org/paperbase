Chat with your papers

Grounded answers across your library and the literature.

Paperbase Chat is a reading room for scientific work. It lets you bring your own documents, search across indexed literature, and stay grounded in source material while asking broad questions.

The interface is intentionally spare. Start with a question, attach your own files, or use one of the examples below to open a new conversation.

Upload and tag documents

PDF, DOCX, PPTX, Markdown, and text files become part of your private chat library after login.
